# Grampians additions – September 2026

Eight destinations, represented by nine forecast entries. Upper and Lower Centurion are separate siblings under `gramps-main` so the lower tier's partial closure is explicit. Tribute remains a combined entry with mixed aspect and shade because its upper walls and ravine do not have one representative exposure.

## Evidence and confidence

Access checked against [Parks Victoria's current Gariwerd guidance](https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/grampians-national-park/rock-climbing-in-gariwerd) and its June 2026 designated-area list on 12 September 2026. Designated status does not override temporary park or road closures.

| Andersens West | Approximate centre of DCA 10 on Parks Victoria Map B | Multiple boulder faces and rooflets |
| Venus Baths | Mountain Project: -37.13811, 142.51449; 256 m | Multiple faces and limited tree cover; UKC also lists all aspects |
| Bad Moon Rising Wall | Approximate DCA 38 location on Parks Victoria Map B | NE is a map-derived working estimate; Chockstone describes sun through much of the day |
| Grey and Green Walls | Approximate DCA 20 location on Parks Victoria Map B. Do not use Mountain Project's inherited amphitheatre pin as a surveyed cliff location | NW is a map-derived working estimate; corners vary |

Map-derived coordinates are rounded forecast reference points, not surveyed locations or navigation instructions. Map B estimates use the mapped Trackside location and the printed scale as a reference; Map A locates Centurion relative to the same northern area. Elevations other than Watchtower and Venus Baths are approximate. Ideal temperature ranges, drying ratings and broad shade periods are initial modelling estimates, not measured microclimate data. Refine with local observations rather than treating these numbers as verified guidebook facts.

## Access details

- Lower Centurion: Damage Done and routes north of it are closed. Routes south of and including In Plain Sight remain open. No all-year closure flag is applied to the permitted southern sector.
- Upper Centurion: access remains via the south end near Winged Corpse; do not cross the closed lower section.
- Andersens West: scope is limited to the fourteen named blocks in Parks Victoria's guidance, not the whole historical Andersens area.
- Grey and Green Walls: approach via Central Buttress; no onward access into Taipan's designated area from these walls.
- No recurring nesting closure has been invented for these entries. Temporary notices and active nest restrictions must be checked with Parks Victoria.
